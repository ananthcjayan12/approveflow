# ApproveFlow — Motion Graphics Script (Draft v2)

**Concept:** A short story about an agency owner's week. First the struggle, then the relief. The features show up as the things that end each pain, not as a feature list.
**Length:** ~100 seconds · **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps
**Look:** flat, friendly character illustration combined with simplified UI. Brand colours are cream `#f4eddf`, ink `#1c1b18` and yellow `#ffc53d`, with green / orange / red for status.
**Colour arc:** Part 1 is washed-out and grey-blue, with red notification dots everywhere. When ApproveFlow arrives, the yellow floods in, and from then on everything is warm cream and yellow.
**Audio:** Part 1 has a slightly frantic ticking beat and ping sounds. It cuts to silence at the turning point, then warm, upbeat music plays for Part 2. Captions carry the story, so it works on mute.

---

## Cast

- **The agency owner** runs *Pixel Agency*, a three-person design studio. They're talented, busy and running on coffee.
- **The client** is at *SmileCraft Dental*. They're kind but busy, and they reply from their phone between patients.

---

## PART 1 — THE STRUGGLE (0:00–0:38)

### Scene 1 · Monday, 9:02 AM (0:00–0:06)
- **Visual:** The owner at their desk, proudly hitting "send" on the October content: 12 Instagram posts, a carousel and a reel. The files leave as a messy email with 14 attachments.
- **Caption:** *Monday. October content, done. Sent to the client.* ✉️
- **Sound:** a satisfied "whoosh".

### Scene 2 · The feedback arrives… from everywhere (0:06–0:16)
- **Visual:** Their screen fills up with pings, faster and faster:
  - WhatsApp: *"Can the number be bigger?"* (Which post? Which number?)
  - Email reply-all from the receptionist: *"Love it!!"*
  - A blurry phone photo of the screen with a finger pointing at… something.
  - A voice note: *"at around the middle of the video…"* (Which video? Which middle?)
  - Email: *"Use the new logo"* (There is no new logo in the thread.)
- **Caption:** *"Which post?" "Which number?" "Which version?!"*
- **Visual beat:** The owner's head tilts further with every ping.

### Scene 3 · The version maze (0:16–0:24)
- **Visual:** The camera zooms into their desktop folder: `post1_final.jpg`, `post1_final_v2.jpg`, `post1_FINAL_approved.jpg`, `post1_final_v2_USE_THIS.jpg`. The files shuffle around like a shell game.
- **Caption:** *Wait… which one did they actually approve?*

### Scene 4 · The chase (0:24–0:32)
- **Visual:** A calendar flips Tue → Wed → Thu. Each day The owner'sends a nervous follow-up: *"Just checking in!"*, *"Gentle reminder 🙂"*, *"Hi again!"*. Silence. The post date on the calendar glows red and edges closer.
- **Caption:** *Thursday. Still waiting. Launch is Monday.*

### Scene 5 · 11:48 PM (0:32–0:38)
- **Visual:** The office is dark and only the screen lights their face. Cold coffee, 37 unread messages. They slump onto the desk. The frantic beat stops dead, and the only sound left is a clock ticking.
- **Caption:** *There has to be a better way.*

---

## TURNING POINT (0:38–0:44)

### Scene 6 · The switch
- **Visual:** A small yellow square (the ApproveFlow mark) blinks on in the corner of their dark screen. They lift their head. The square expands and floods the whole frame with yellow and warm cream, and the grey is gone.
- **Caption (serif, big):** **Client approval without the chaos.**
- **Sound:** a single warm chime, then the music begins.

---

## PART 2 — THE RELIEF (0:44–1:34)

### Scene 7 · Next month. Monday, 9:02 AM (0:44–0:54)
- **Visual:** The same desk, now sunny. The owner drags October's files into ApproveFlow (**Upload**), including the big reel, whose progress bar zips across in segments. They pick SmileCraft Dental → *November Content*, tick the assets, and switch on **Automatic reminders** (24h, 72h). Then they press **Send for approval**.
- **Visual:** The upload turns into **one secure link**, which flies off to the client.
- **Caption:** *Upload once. Send one link.*

### Scene 8 · The client, between patients (0:54–1:10)
- **Visual:** The client, at the clinic, opens the link on their phone. **No login screen** appears. It opens straight to *"November Content · Reviewing as client"*.
  - They tap the phone number, and a yellow marker **①** appears: *"Make this bigger"*.
  - They box the logo: **②** *"Use the new logo (attached)"*.
  - They swipe to slide 3 of the carousel and pin a note there: **③**.
  - They scrub the reel and highlight **0:04–0:09**: *"Slow this bit down"*.
  - They tap **Request changes**, and all four notes fly off together as one tidy bundle.
- **Caption:** *No login. No guessing. Every note, exactly where it belongs.*
- **Beat:** The client smiles, pockets the phone, and walks back to their patient. The whole thing took 90 seconds.

### Scene 9 · The owner gets it all, perfectly clear (1:10–1:18)
- **Visual:** The owner's screen shows the four notes pinned right on the designs, numbered, with nothing to decode. They fix them and upload, and **V2 · current** slides in over **V1 · changes requested**.
- **Caption:** *Clear feedback. Clean versions.*

### Scene 10 · No more chasing (1:18–1:24)
- **Visual:** A clock spins forward 24 hours. A friendly reminder slides into the client's inbox by itself, while the owner leans back sipping a *hot* coffee.
- **Caption:** *ApproveFlow sends the reminders. You don't have to.*

### Scene 11 · Approved ✓ (1:24–1:34)
- **Visual:** The client taps **Approve** → *"This records approval for the current version"* → **Confirm**. A big green ✓ stamp lands on the post, and the Activity feed ticks in: *Viewed · Changes requested · V2 uploaded · Reminder sent · Approved ✓*.
- **Visual:** The owner's dashboard: every project is green and on time. They close the laptop. It's 5:30 PM and daylight outside.
- **Caption:** *Every version. Every decision. On record. Home by 5:30.*

- **Caption (serif):** **ApproveFlow** · *Get to "approved" without the chaos.*
- **Button:** **Get started free** · URL: `[your URL]`

## Feature coverage (woven into the story)

| Pain in Part 1 | Relief in Part 2 | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Messy email with 14 attachments | Scene 7 | Upload (including large videos in parts), projects and clients |
| Client can't log in or won't | Scene 8 | No-login secure review link |
| "Which post? Which number?" | Scene 8 | Pins, boxes, drawings, carousel slides, video ranges |
| Feedback scattered and drip-fed | Scene 8 | Request changes sends every note together |
| `final_v2_USE_THIS.jpg` | Scene 9 | Version history (V1 → V2) |
| Nervous follow-up emails | Scene 10 | Automatic reminders |
| "which one did they approve?" | Scene 11 | Approval per version plus audit trail |

Storage, billing and branding are dropped to keep the story tight. They could return as a 3-second montage before the end card if you want them.
